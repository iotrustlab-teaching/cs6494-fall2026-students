## Rules of engagement

All adversarial activity stays inside explicitly authorized course environments, accounts,
networks, devices, and time windows. Do not scan, probe, attack, or attempt to access live
infrastructure, public networks, personal devices, or anything outside the written scope. Follow
the platform rules, safety instructions, stop conditions, data-handling rules, and reset procedures
for whichever environment an activity uses.

Unsafe or unauthorized behavior can mean immediate loss of platform access and referral under
University policy. If you are unsure whether something is in scope, ask before doing it.

## Generative AI

AI tools are both objects of study and, in designated activities, tools you may be required or
permitted to use. Unless an assignment says otherwise, you may use AI for brainstorming, debugging,
and editing **only when** you disclose substantive use, verify the output, cite sources
independently, and remain able to explain and reproduce your work.

Do not submit unreviewed AI-generated work, use AI to bypass an individual assessment, expose
confidential course or testbed information to external services, or grant an AI tool access beyond
an assignment's rules of engagement. Assignment-specific instructions take precedence.
