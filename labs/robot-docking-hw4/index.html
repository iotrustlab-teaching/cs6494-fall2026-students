3 · Check the requirements

Useful service: reach within 0.5 m by second 5 and remain there for three consecutive one-second samples.

Safe approach: whenever a sampled position is inside that region during seconds 0–5, speed must be at most 0.2 m/s.

For the Monday formula exercise, edit student_spec.py and run .venv/bin/python hw4.py check-spec. That practice formula does not change the fixed results above.

Show the exact STL formulas and interpretation
RTAMT uses discrete time at one-second spacing. The speed formula uses dimensionless distance and speed terms scaled by 0.5 m and 0.2 m/s; its score is not added to a margin in metres. At zero robustness, inspect strict versus inclusive predicates. If the robot never enters, the conditional speed rule is logically true, while its antecedent is marked NOT EXERCISED; that does not demonstrate safe docking.
